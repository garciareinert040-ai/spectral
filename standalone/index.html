Notes

  • Headlines and summaries remain © their publishers; every card links to the original article.
  • Feeds are fetched in your browser. Because of CORS, requests fall back through public relays (allorigins.win, corsproxy.io, codetabs.com) — only feed URLs pass through them.
  • Reuters, AP and AFP headlines arrive via the Google News RSS proxy; their direct APIs are enterprise-licensed.
  • Articles persist in IndexedDB on this device. Updates are manual — nothing syncs in the background.
